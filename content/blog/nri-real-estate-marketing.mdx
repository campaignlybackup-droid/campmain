Non-resident Indians in the Gulf, North America and elsewhere are an important audience for many Indian residential projects. Marketing to them is not the same as marketing locally: they cannot drop in for a site visit, they are often in a different time zone, and they need more reassurance before committing.

## Who are you targeting?

Treat NRI buyers as several segments:

| Segment | Typical motivation | What they need to see |
|---|---|---|
| Gulf-based professionals | Investment, future return home | Price, payment plan, rental potential |
| North American families | Home for parents, future retirement | Location quality, builder track record, management services |
| Recent emigrants | Keep a foothold in their home city | Familiar locations, simple buying process |

## How should campaigns be targeted?

- **By country, then language.** Target people living in the UAE, USA, Canada or the UK, and combine with language and interest signals relevant to the home region of your project.
- **By project location.** A project in Kochi will appeal to a different diaspora than one in Pune.
- **By timing.** Diaspora interest often rises around festivals and holidays, when people plan trips home.

In the USA and Canada, campaigns advertising housing on Meta must be declared under the Housing Special Ad Category, which restricts targeting options. Confirm how Meta classifies your campaign before launch.

## What should the landing page include?

Remote buyers need more evidence than local ones:

- The project's RERA registration number and the regulator's website, as Section 11(2) of the RERA Act requires for advertisements
- A clear explanation of the buying process for NRIs, including documentation and payment
- Video walkthroughs of the site and sample flat
- The developer's track record of completed projects
- Contact options that work across time zones, including WhatsApp

## How do you follow up across time zones?

India is ahead of the Gulf by 1.5 hours, and roughly 9.5 to 13.5 hours ahead of North America depending on the city and daylight saving. A lead from Toronto submitted in their evening arrives in the Indian morning.

- Reply instantly with automation, so the buyer knows they were heard.
- Ask when they prefer a call, and offer slots in their time zone.
- Offer live video site visits as an alternative to a physical visit.
- Keep a sales team member available in the evening, India time, for North American calls.

## How do you measure success?

The same as any real estate campaign: cost per qualified lead, cost per video or physical site visit, and cost per booking. NRI cycles can be longer, so track leads over several months.
