The point of a qualification flow is simple: answer every lead while they are still interested, find out quickly whether they are a fit, and put the good ones in front of a salesperson with context. Everything else is detail.

## What should the flow do first?

1. **Acknowledge instantly.** Thank them, confirm what they asked about, and say what happens next.
2. **Ask permission to ask.** "Can I ask three quick questions so the right person calls you?"
3. **Qualify.** Two to four questions, one at a time, with buttons rather than free text where possible.
4. **Route.** Qualified leads go to the right person; others get useful information and a way to come back.
5. **Record.** Every answer is written to the CRM.

## Which questions should you ask?

Use the criteria your sales team already uses to decide whether a lead is worth a call. Typical examples:

| Industry | Questions |
|---|---|
| Real estate | Budget range, configuration, timeline to buy, self-use or investment |
| Education | Course of interest, current qualification, intake, city |
| B2B services | Company size, role, problem to solve, timeline |
| Healthcare | Service needed, preferred location, preferred time (never medical details) |

Keep each question answerable in one tap. Every extra question loses some people, so ask only what changes the next step.

## When should a person take over?

- The lead is qualified and ready to talk
- The lead asks something the flow cannot answer well
- The lead is unhappy or confused
- The lead asks for a person, at any point

The handover should include a short summary so the salesperson does not repeat the questions.

## What rules apply?

The flow must run on the official WhatsApp Business Platform. Free-form replies are allowed for 24 hours after the customer's last message; after that you need approved templates. Business-initiated follow-ups need prior opt-in. See [WhatsApp Business API vs app](/blog/whatsapp-business-api-vs-whatsapp-business-app/) for the details.

## Where does AI help?

AI is useful for understanding free-text answers, summarising conversations for the sales team and answering common questions from approved information. It should not invent prices, availability or promises. Keep a human route open and review transcripts regularly.

## How do you measure the flow?

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Time to first response | Whether leads are answered while still engaged |
| Completion rate | Whether the questions are too many or unclear |
| Qualified rate | Whether ads are attracting the right people |
| Handover to contact time | Whether sales follow up fast enough |
| Qualified to sale rate | Whether the criteria predict real buyers |
