The UAE is one of the most international populations in the world. That makes the language decision for advertising less obvious than it looks: Arabic is the official language, but English is widely used in daily business and by most residents.

## Who are you trying to reach?

Start with the buyer, not the language:

| Audience | Usual language preference |
|---|---|
| Emirati nationals | Arabic, often bilingual |
| Arab expatriates | Arabic, often bilingual |
| South Asian, Western and other expatriates | English, sometimes other languages such as Hindi, Malayalam or Tagalog |
| Business decision-makers | English is common, Arabic valued for some sectors |

## When does Arabic matter most?

- When your buyers are Emirati or Arab expatriate families
- In categories where cultural fit and trust are central, such as family services, healthcare and some premium segments
- For organisations working closely with government entities
- When competitors in the category advertise only in English, and Arabic stands out

## Why not just translate?

Translated ads tend to read as translated. Arabic creative should be written for its audience: the message, references, tone and sometimes the offer itself may need to change. Layout also changes, because Arabic reads right to left, which affects how text and visuals are placed.

## How do you run both?

1. **Target by language**, using platform language settings, rather than showing both languages to everyone.
2. **Match the landing page.** An Arabic ad should lead to an Arabic page.
3. **Compare on the same business metric**, such as cost per qualified lead, not on clicks.
4. **Respond in the same language.** If a lead writes in Arabic, the follow-up should be in Arabic.

## Which platforms matter?

Google and Meta are central, and Snapchat and TikTok are also widely used for reaching younger audiences in the UAE. Each supports language targeting, though how it works differs by platform.

## What rules apply?

Since 2025, individuals who publish promotional content on social media in the UAE need an Advertiser Permit from the UAE Media Council, so check permits before commissioning creator or influencer content. Property advertising in Dubai needs a Dubai Land Department permit. See the [UAE Media Council announcement](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/bkxzoe1-uae-media-council-launches-advertiser-permit) for details.

## The takeaway

Most UAE businesses need English for reach and Arabic for depth with specific audiences. Write for each audience, measure on outcomes, and let the data shift the budget between them.
