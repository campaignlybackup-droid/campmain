An admissions team can have its best month for lead volume and its worst month for enrolments at the same time. That happens when campaigns are judged on cost per lead, because the cheapest leads are often the least likely to apply.

## What is cost per admission?

**Cost per admission** = total marketing spend for an intake ÷ number of students admitted from that spend.

It is the education equivalent of customer acquisition cost. It forces every other metric to answer the question: did this help fill seats?

## Why does cost per lead mislead?

- Easy forms and broad offers generate many enquiries from people who are only browsing.
- Some enquiries are parents, some students, some are duplicates across courses.
- Counsellors spend time calling people who will never apply, and miss the ones who would.
- Ad platforms optimise toward whatever you call a conversion. If that is "form submitted", they find form-submitters.

## Which stages should you track?

| Stage | Definition | Metric |
|---|---|---|
| Enquiry | Any form, call or WhatsApp message | Cost per enquiry |
| Verified | Contactable, genuine enquiry | Verification rate |
| Qualified | Eligible, interested in a current intake | Cost per qualified lead |
| Counselled | Spoke to a counsellor | Counselling rate |
| Applied | Application submitted | Cost per application |
| Admitted | Seat confirmed | Cost per admission |

The conversion rate between each stage tells you where the problem is. A low verification rate points to form or targeting issues. A low counselling rate points to slow follow-up. A low application rate after counselling points to offer, fees or fit.

## How do you optimise for admissions?

1. **Agree definitions.** Write down what qualified means for each programme.
2. **Connect the CRM.** Every enquiry needs its source recorded so outcomes can be traced to campaigns.
3. **Respond fast.** Automated WhatsApp replies confirm interest and eligibility within minutes, especially in peak season.
4. **Send outcomes back.** Import qualified, applied and admitted events into Google Ads and send them to Meta through the Conversions API, so bidding learns which students enrol.
5. **Judge campaigns at the right stage.** Early in the season, use qualified leads as the signal; at the end, compare campaigns on cost per application and per admission.

## What about the long admission cycle?

Admission decisions can take months, which is longer than ad platforms' optimisation windows. That is why earlier-stage signals, such as qualified lead or counselling completed, are useful as optimisation events, while cost per admission is the final scorecard for each intake.

## The takeaway

Cost per lead is a cost of activity. Cost per admission is a cost of results. Institutions that track the full funnel spend less per enrolled student, because they stop paying for the wrong enquiries.
