"Should we get the WhatsApp API?" usually means "we are getting more messages than we can handle". Whether the answer is yes depends on volume, how many people reply, and whether you need WhatsApp connected to your other systems.

## What is the difference?

| Feature | WhatsApp Business app | WhatsApp Business Platform (API) |
|---|---|---|
| Cost to use | Free | Per delivered template message, plus any provider fees |
| Set-up | Install on a phone | Meta Business verification and a provider or direct Cloud API set-up |
| Who replies | People, on linked devices | People through a shared inbox, plus automation |
| Automation | Greeting, away and quick replies | Full automation, chatbots and AI qualification |
| Integrations | Very limited | CRM, website, ad platforms, any system with an API |
| Bulk messaging | Limited broadcast lists | Template campaigns to opted-in contacts at scale |

## What rules come with the API?

- **Opt-in:** you need the customer's permission before sending them business-initiated messages.
- **The 24-hour window:** after a customer messages you, you can reply freely for 24 hours. Outside that window, you must use a pre-approved message template.
- **Templates:** Meta reviews templates and categorises them as marketing, utility or authentication.
- **Pricing:** since 1 July 2025, Meta charges per delivered template message rather than per conversation. Rates vary by country and category, and replies inside the 24-hour window are not charged. Check [Meta's pricing page](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing) for current rates.

## Can you keep your existing number?

Often yes. Meta's coexistence option allows many numbers already on the WhatsApp Business app to connect to the API while staying active in the app, with chat history kept. Not every number or provider supports it, so check before migrating.

## When should you move to the API?

Move when one or more of these is true:

- Several people need to reply from one number
- You receive more enquiries than your team can answer quickly
- You want leads from ads or your website to receive an instant WhatsApp reply
- You need conversations recorded in your CRM
- You want to send order updates, reminders or campaigns to opted-in customers

Stay on the app when one or two people handle all conversations comfortably and you do not need integrations.

## What should you avoid?

Unofficial tools that automate the regular WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app. They can get the number banned, taking every customer conversation with it. Automation should run only on the official platform.

## How to switch

1. Verify your business in Meta Business Manager.
2. Choose a provider or set up the Cloud API directly.
3. Decide whether to use coexistence or migrate the number fully.
4. Write and submit your first templates.
5. Build the flows and connect your CRM.
