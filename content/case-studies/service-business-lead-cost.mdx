## The challenge

The business was generating leads, but each one cost more every month. Every ad sent traffic straight to a form with no qualification step, so low-intent enquiries were mixed in with real buyers and the sales team spent its time sorting them out.

## What we did

- **Split the markets.** India and the UAE got separate campaigns, audiences, messaging and landing pages.
- **Built creative for each buyer stage.** We tested more than 25 angles across awareness, consideration and decision.
- **Added a qualification step.** A short multi-step form now filters out low-intent enquiries before they reach the team.
- **Optimised every week.** We cut failing segments, scaled the winners and adjusted bids on the evidence.

## What changed

On the same budget, cost per lead fell 42%. The business now generates 2.8 times as many qualified leads, and cost per sale is down 55%. The lesson: leads become cheaper and better when the funnel is designed first and the budget scaled second.
