## The challenge

The store had traffic but too few sales. Product pages lost visitors as soon as they arrived, the five-step checkout lost buyers at payment, and key pages carried no proof to build trust.

## What we did

- **Found the leaks.** We used heatmaps and session recordings to find the points where buyers dropped off.
- **Shortened checkout.** Five steps became two, and every unnecessary field was removed.
- **Rebuilt product pages.** We designed them mobile first, with larger images, clearer benefits and proof placed where buyers decide.
- **Tested every change.** Each new version was A/B tested against the old one, and only proven winners went live.

## What changed

Conversion rate rose from 2% to 7% and bounce rate fell 28%. Monthly revenue grew from $100K to $350K on the same ad spend. Fix the funnel first, then scale the traffic.
