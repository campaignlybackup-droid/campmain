## The challenge

The brand's Meta campaigns had stalled at 2.8x ROAS. Adding budget only brought diminishing returns, the same ads had been running for months, and new creative went live at random rather than through a testing plan.

## What we did

- **Studied the winners.** We found the patterns shared by the creative that was already working.
- **Built a testing framework.** We launched more than 20 new variants a month, testing hooks, proof and product angles.
- **Widened the audience on data.** We built lookalikes from the highest-value customers and expanded in stages.
- **Scaled with discipline.** Weak ads came out every week and budget moved to whatever held its return.

## What changed

ROAS rose to 5x while ad budget grew 3.5 times, and the campaigns stayed profitable throughout. Conversion rate improved 2.4 times. Scale came from systematic testing, not from bigger budgets alone.
