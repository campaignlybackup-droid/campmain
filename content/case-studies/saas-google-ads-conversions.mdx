## The challenge

The SaaS company had plenty of search traffic and very few leads. Competitive clicks were expensive, broad match was buying low-intent queries, and generic landing pages did not match what people had searched for.

## What we did

- **Cut the waste.** We removed more than 200 low-intent broad-match keywords and focused on exact and phrase match with purchase intent.
- **Matched pages to intent.** We built 15 landing pages, one for each major keyword cluster.
- **Fixed measurement.** GA4 and Google Ads conversion tracking were rebuilt so bidding could optimise for real outcomes.
- **Managed bids weekly.** Bids were adjusted by keyword and by market based on conversion performance.

## What changed

Conversions rose 250% and cost per acquisition fell 35%. The lead-to-sale rate went from 18% to 42%. Precision beat volume: fewer, better clicks produced far more revenue.
