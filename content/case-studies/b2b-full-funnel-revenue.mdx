## The challenge

The firm's Meta ads, Google Ads and email all ran separately, with no shared data. Prospects saw several touchpoints but never a coordinated journey, follow-up was manual, and revenue swung from month to month.

## What we did

- **Connected the data.** GA4, the CRM and both ad platforms were linked so every interaction was tracked across channels.
- **Designed one funnel.** Awareness, consideration and conversion each got a clear job on Meta, Google and email.
- **Automated the follow-up.** Retargeting, email sequences and CRM triggers ensured every lead got a response.
- **Allocated budget on evidence.** Each week we moved spend between channels based on conversion data from all sources.

## What changed

Monthly revenue grew from $120K to $480K in twelve months, and conversion rate tripled. Revenue also became predictable enough to forecast, because every channel now feeds the others instead of running alone.
