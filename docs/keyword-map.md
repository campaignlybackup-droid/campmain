# Keyword map: campaignlymedia.com

Status: **Draft for approval (Phase 1)**. Prepared 11 November 2026.

## Read this first: limits of this document

1. **I have no search volume or keyword difficulty data.** I have no access to Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush or Search Console for this domain. Every "Difficulty" and "Volume" label below is an **estimate**. I based each one on what currently ranks: who the competitors are, whether directories and listicles hold the top spots, and how many established agencies target the exact phrase. Before Phase 3 copywriting, check the primaries in Keyword Planner (free with a Google Ads account) or Search Console. I can then re-rank the choices.
2. **The SERP checks were run from a US-located search tool.** Results for India, Canada and UAE queries are an approximation of what a searcher in Mumbai, Toronto or Dubai would see. Local results (Map Pack, local directories) will differ. Re-check the top 10 from each market, using a VPN or the Keyword Planner location setting, before final copy.
3. **Rule applied: one primary keyword per URL.** No primary keyword appears twice. Where a phrase is a secondary on one page, it is never the primary of another. The cannibalisation check at the end lists the pairs that sit close together and explains how they differ.

### Brand-search finding (important)

A search for **"Campaignly Media"** currently returns **Campaignly Group (campaignlygroup.com)**, an unrelated digital communications firm for political campaigns. No result for campaignlymedia.com appeared. Brand SERPs are therefore contested from day one. Mitigations, all already in the brief or added to post-launch:

- Organization schema with `name` "Campaignly", `alternateName` ["Campaignly Media", "CampaignlyMedia"], a consistent `url`, `logo` and `sameAs`.
- "Campaignly Media" in the Home title tag (not only "Campaignly"), because "Campaignly" alone is the more contested string.
- A Google Business Profile, a LinkedIn company page, and Clutch and DesignRush profiles, all pointing to campaignlymedia.com. These feed `sameAs` and the Knowledge Graph.
- The About page opens with an unambiguous definition (advertising-first growth partner for businesses, not political campaigns). That way search engines and AI answer engines disambiguate.

## Difficulty legend (all estimates)

- **High:** top 10 dominated by established agencies, networks and directories. Not realistic for a new domain within 12 months without authority building.
- **Medium:** mix of mid-size agencies and thin pages. Realistic in 6 to 12 months with strong content and some links.
- **Low:** long-tail, thin or off-intent results. Realistic in 3 to 6 months.
